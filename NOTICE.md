# Attribution and modification notice


Complete paged rule review and streamed CSV audit are newly authored port interfaces over the existing Rule::Runner adaptation. Iterator-based collection preserves prior valid evaluation results while exposing all proposed changes with scope/revision-bound approval, stable review-only split references and explicit runtime/output bounds. It does not add a provider, financial action or persisted data format.

This application is an independent TypeScript adaptation of the personal-finance workflows in Sure, the community fork of Maybe Finance.

The Rails server, Active Record persistence, Redis jobs, and Rails-rendered interface are replaced here with a React/TypeScript interface, a Cloudflare Worker, D1 persistence, and ChatGPT Sites identity. The transaction kind names and exclusion semantics derive from Sure's Transaction model. Split grouping follows Entry.split!, and the recurrence date engine and payment-history behavior follow RecurringTransaction::Schedule, RecurringOccurrence, and RecurringAllocation, adapted for explicit manual actions in Sites. The v2 NDJSON migration adapter follows Family::DataExporter and the signed-flow/valuation semantics of Balance::ForwardCalculator. The manual portfolio engine adapts the cash/non-cash balance separation, average-cost pool and proportional lots in Holding::LotBook and Holding::ForwardCalculator, including fee and corporate-action handling. The hierarchical budget engine adapts Budget, BudgetCategory, Budget::RolloverCalculator and IncomeStatement, with explicit private account scopes and dated periods. Goal pooling, reserve targets, lifecycle snapshots and consumption follow Goal and GoalAccount, with integer largest-remainder allocation for shared shortfalls. The ordered transaction-rule engine adapts Rule::Runner, condition filters, scalar/tag executors and SplitTransaction, with previewed atomic D1 writes and explicit currency scopes. Manual statement comparisons, signed balance reconciliation and transaction check states adapt AccountStatement, Account::ReconciliationManager, Account::ReconciliationIndicator and Entry, with previewed adjustments and guarded before-image restoration. Private statement originals adapt AccountStatement attachment type/size limits and family checksum deduplication to owner-scoped R2 storage, recoverable operation states and streaming ZIP backups. Pending-savings reminders and matching adapt GoalPledge and GoalPledge::Reconciler, including their FIFO order, tolerances, expiry/extension and positive balance-delta behavior, with request-driven expiry, guarded historical previews and durable source snapshots. Contribution-based goal progress adapts Goal#net_contributed_for and Balance::BaseCalculator#market_value_change_on_date through an exact telescoping flow calculation, with explicit dated checkpoints and warnings for incomplete holding/movement history. Dated rate lookup adapts ExchangeRate::Provided's five-day prior-quote convention, with exact rational inverses; cross-currency principal and separate source/destination fee treatment adapt TransferCreator. Cross-currency goal allocation, progress, usage and pending-savings matching adapt Goal::CurrencyConverter, Goal#consume! and GoalPledge#matches? with immutable applied quotes and explicit native-unit rounding. Expense-based reserves adapt IncomeStatement::FamilyStats through the workspace-currency median. Cross-currency budget reporting adapts IncomeStatement::ScopedTransactionsQuery, Budget#available_cash, Budget#earmarked_for_goals and Goal#backing_within, with exact split apportionment and explicit incomplete-carry tracking. Cross-currency recurring allocation and native capacity adapt RecurringTransaction::Allocator#resolve_amounts and #guard_entry_capacity!, with dated/posted quote snapshots and guarded manual account entry creation. Decimal arithmetic, user-owned D1 tables and manual input workflows are implemented in TypeScript. Default category colors derive from Sure's category palette. The interface and default sample dataset are newly authored for this adaptation.

Account subtype catalogs, regional choice order, derived investment/depository/crypto tax treatment and tax-aware spending scopes adapt the pinned Accountable, Investment, Depository, Crypto, TaxTreatable, Family#tax_advantaged_account_ids and IncomeStatement::ScopedTransactionsQuery methods. Catalog values are source application metadata; the port does not calculate taxes. Archive-date metadata and historical cutoff rules adapt the pinned Account state transitions and BalanceSheet/Balance::ChartSeriesBuilder account windows.

Manual card terms, month-end billing dates, payment status, simple monthly interest, minimum-payment comparison and stored schedule selection adapt the pinned CreditCard model. Closed debt uses this port’s normalized signed-balance/end-of-day-observation adapter rather than summing raw Valuation entries; missing cached payment hints use explicitly labelled recurrence derivation. Bank/provider balance interpretation is excluded.

Manual property/vehicle fields, measurement display, square-foot conversion, purchase precedence and reviewed vehicle purchase-anchor/current-value preservation adapt the pinned Property, Vehicle, Measurement, Address and Account::OpeningBalanceManager models. Separate normalized opening values and missing provenance are explicit port adaptations. Property/vehicle valuation providers and enrichment jobs are not connected.

Valuation-only balance handling for OtherAsset/OtherLiability, alongside Property/Vehicle, follows pinned Account#balance_type and Balance::BaseCalculator flows_for_date/derive_cash_balance/derive_non_cash_balance. The accounting-impact comparison retains the port’s prior formula as read-only evidence; original records remain intact.

Recorded future-entry projections adapt the pinned Account scheduled-entry flow/conversion/total/projection methods and ActivityFeedData’s future-date helper, using the port’s current physical-value reader and normalized signed currency amounts. Future observations remain separate evidence. The optional loan principal estimate reuses the port’s reviewed payment replay; it is distinct from Sure’s gross scheduled-movement projection.

Security movement cash/fee entry adapts the pinned Trade::CreateForm sweep calculations, Balance forward cash/flow handling, Trade internal-movement/no-realized-gain rules and Holding’s inbound-unknown basis behavior. Explicit net amounts, immutable applied FX and the legacy blank-CSV zero default use this port’s existing accounting and recovery adapters.

The complete normalized trade-label catalog follows pinned Transaction/Trade::ACTIVITY_LABELS. Priced basis/disposal handling for Contribution, Withdrawal, Exchange and Other adapts Holding::TradeCalculatorHelpers and Trade internal-movement/realized-gain/basis-currency methods, with explicit recorded cash and immutable applied rational FX in the port. Portfolio::FlowClassifier supplies the separate source flow semantics.

Reviewed merchant merging adapts pinned Merchant::Merger, Entry.mark_user_modified_for_transactions! and Rule.replace_merchant_references! to private owned merchants, manual merchant-field protections and atomic D1 writes. Transaction/split references and supported simple/compound/direct/split rule references move while original configurations remain intact. Preserving and reassigning the port's recurring definitions, occurrences and payments deliberately differs from upstream Merchant destruction callbacks. Global provider merchants and notification baselines are outside this adapter.

Reviewed category merging adapts pinned Category::Merger and Transaction.reassign_category! hierarchy/reparenting, inclusive budget allocation sums, target-envelope identity/settings and affected-entry protection to guarded D1 writes. Stable source-ID ordering makes the no-target envelope winner deterministic. The port adds reviewed transaction/split manual category locks and supported category-rule rewriting, and preserves recurring payment/lifecycle graphs. Original sources remain intact; retired budget rollover/carry settings are exposed before confirmation. Full Rails SQL locking/cache/provider-claim/destruction behavior is outside the controlled adapter.

The manual XLSX-to-reviewed-transaction converter is a port extension over the private file vault and existing staged CSV workflow; the pinned Sure vault reports XLSX metadata extraction unsupported. It reads stored OOXML values with explicit Excel-date and cached-formula choices, never evaluates formulas, and retains immutable original/derived checksums and worksheet row provenance. XML parsing uses saxes 6.0.0 (ISC, including its upstream notices) and xmlchars 2.2.0 (MIT). Complete notices are distributed at /saxes-LICENSE.txt and /xmlchars-LICENSE.txt and in the corresponding source archive. No PDF/AI extraction or external workbook link fetching is added.

Regular-expression transaction rules adapt the pinned raw-field matching/review workflow with RE2JS 2.10.0 (MIT) and explicit compile/workspace/run bounds. RE2 syntax and Unicode folding are distinct from PostgreSQL ARE/locale behavior; source patterns remain disabled pending manual review. Port CSV exports name their dialect and use port-specific condition types. The full RE2JS notice is distributed at /re2js-LICENSE.txt and in the corresponding source archive.
